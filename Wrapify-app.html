Turn Your Spotify Archive into Wrapify Insights

Drop in your full Spotify streaming history and get instant breakdowns of your top songs and artists by year

1. Add your Spotify data folder

To start, you need to download your full Spotify streaming history. You can do this by going to: Spotify > Privacy Settings > Download your data, where you specifically chose Extended streaming history.

Steps to follow:

  1. Request and download your Extended streaming history (my_spotify_data.zip) from Spotify.
  2. Extract / unzip my_spotify_data.zip on your computer or phone.
  3. Open the extracted my_spotify_data folder and select the Spotify Extended Streaming History folder when uploading to Wrapify.

Your files are never sent to a server. All parsing and analysis happens locally in your browser using JavaScript.
