Paint2 is a complete redo of one of my very first coding projects. The original, Paint1, was created in April of 2019 for my first coding course in college. Built in Java, Paint1 is meant to calculate how many gallons of paint are required to paint a wall. However, it being my first project, it is both rudimentary and unfunctional. there are naming issues with some elements and others are called in the wrong spots. When corrected, it works in a very basic way.

Finally, Paint2 also removes the surface area that is not painted, such as windows and doors, from the total wall area calculation. so if you have a wall with a door and two windows, Paint2 will subtract the area of those elements from the total wall area before calculating. What's more, I ensured that negative values aren't possible, meaning if you input a window larger than the orignal wall input, an error is thrown.
